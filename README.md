### 📌 Overview & SEO Summary
Welcome to the ultimate curated directory of **all-in-one SMB CRM platforms**, **open-source CRM frameworks**, and **small business relationship management tools**. Whether you are looking for enterprise-grade commercial solutions (such as *Salesforce Pro Suite*, *HubSpot Starter CRM*, and *Zoho CRM Plus*), or self-hostable open-source alternatives (like *EspoCRM*, *SuiteCRM*, and *Twenty*), this list covers category leaders, pipeline management, and privacy-respecting small business CRM.

**Key Market Context:**
- **Zoho CRM Plus Starter** is the **most cost-effective all-in-one suite** for small teams at **$20/user/month** (annual), bundling marketing, sales, and customer service into one platform [citation:7][citation:13].
- **HubSpot Starter CRM** raised prices in September 2026 to **$9/seat/month annual** and **$15/seat/month monthly**, but remains the **best free starting point** with its generous free tier [citation:6].
- **Salesforce Pro Suite** starts at **$100/user/month** but **real-year-one costs for a 10-person team range from $39,600 to $74,600** when implementation and add-ons are included [citation:5][citation:8].
- **EspoCRM** is the **leading open-source alternative** for SMBs, offering a **clean interface, powerful in-app administration, and AGPL-3.0 licensing** — though it requires technical expertise to deploy [citation:10].

## 🏢 SaaS / Commercial Platforms

The all-in-one SMB CRM market spans **CRM-led suites** (Salesforce Pro Suite, Zoho CRM Plus) that bundle **sales, marketing, and support into one platform**, **inbound-first platforms** (HubSpot Starter CRM) that excel at **email marketing and lead nurturing**, and **sales-focused tools** (Pipedrive, Copper) that prioritize **pipeline management over breadth**. **Salesforce Pro Suite** starts at **$100/user/month** with **transaction fees** [citation:8]. **HubSpot Starter CRM** costs **$9/seat/month annual** and **$15/seat/month monthly**, with **required onboarding ($1,500–$3,000) on Professional+** [citation:5][citation:6]. **Zoho CRM Plus Starter** is **$20/user/month** (annual) with **no contract lock-in** [citation:7][citation:13]. **Pipedrive** offers an **AI assistant** for lead nurturing and **400+ integrations** [citation:1][citation:9].

## 🔓 Open-Source GitHub Projects

*Sorted by GitHub_Stars_Count (Descending)* 🌟

- **[SuiteCRM](https://github.com/salesagility/SuiteCRM)** [![Stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/salesagility/SuiteCRM?style=social&color=white)](https://github.com/salesagility/SuiteCRM/stargazers)  
  **Award-winning enterprise-class open-source CRM**, AGPL-3.0 licensed. **The successor to SugarCRM Community Edition** — **large user base and many features** [citation:10]. **Workflow automation, modularity, reporting tools, security suite, and customization framework** [citation:10]. **Limitations: outdated codebase, poor user experience, dated design, limited REST API, clunky reports, and subpar mobile UI** [citation:10]. **Best for organizations that need feature breadth and have technical resources to manage legacy code**. 🏛️

- **[EspoCRM](https://github.com/espocrm/espocrm)** [![Stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/espocrm/espocrm?style=social&color=white)](https://github.com/espocrm/espocrm/stargazers)  
  **Open-source CRM with SPA frontend and REST API**, AGPL-3.0 licensed. **Very complete CRM with a clean user interface designed for productivity** [citation:10]. **Powerful in-app administration allows you to customize pretty much everything** [citation:10]. **Roles manager, audit log, email sync, OIDC/LDAP, and rich library of paid extensions** [citation:10]. **Limitations: large codebase, homemade backend and frontend frameworks, limited documentation, and no AI-powered features** [citation:10]. **Best for SMBs that need a complete, customizable CRM and have development resources**. 🎯

- **[Frappe CRM](https://github.com/frappe/crm)** [![Stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/frappe/crm?style=social&color=white)](https://github.com/frappe/crm/stargazers)  
  **Fully featured, modern CRM**, MIT licensed. **Built on the Frappe framework** — **modern architecture with Python backend and Vue.js frontend** [citation:3]. **Part of the ERPNext ecosystem** — **integrates seamlessly with ERPNext's accounting, inventory, and HR modules** [citation:3]. **The most modern open-source CRM architecture**. 🚀

- **[Twenty](https://github.com/twentyhq/twenty)** [![Stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/twentyhq/twenty?style=social&color=white)](https://github.com/twentyhq/twenty/stargazers)  
  **Modern CRM with the flexibility of open source**, AGPL-3.0 licensed. **TypeScript-based** — **built with modern web technologies** [citation:3]. **Designed as a flexible, customizable CRM** for teams that want to build their own workflows [citation:3]. **The most modern TypeScript-native open-source CRM**. ⚡

- **[ERPNext](https://github.com/frappe/erpnext)** [![Stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/frappe/erpnext?style=social&color=white)](https://github.com/frappe/erpnext/stargazers)  
  **All-in-one ERP with robust CRM capabilities**, GPL-3.0 licensed. **The most complete open-source business management platform** — **CRM, accounting, inventory, HR, manufacturing, and more** [citation:3]. **Best for SMBs that need CRM as part of a broader ERP system**. **ERPNext FSM is the leading open-source Field Service Management app** [citation:3]. 🏢

- **[Dolibarr](https://github.com/Dolibarr/dolibarr)** [![Stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/Dolibarr/dolibarr?style=social&color=white)](https://github.com/Dolibarr/dolibarr/stargazers)  
  **Modular ERP and CRM for SMEs**, GPL-3.0 licensed. **Designed specifically for small and medium-sized businesses** [citation:3]. **Modular architecture lets you enable only the features you need** — **CRM, invoicing, inventory, and project management** [citation:3]. **The most SMB-friendly open-source ERP/CRM**. 📦

- **[YetiForce](https://github.com/YetiForceCompany/YetiForceCRM)** [![Stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/YetiForceCompany/YetiForceCRM?style=social&color=white)](https://github.com/YetiForceCompany/YetiForceCRM/stargazers)  
  **Feature-rich CRM with 100+ built-in modules**, open-source. **One of the most innovative CRM systems that supports business processes and allows for customization according to your needs** [citation:18]. **100+ built-in modules** cover nearly every business scenario [citation:3]. **Best for organizations that need extensive out-of-the-box functionality**. ⚙️

- **[OroCRM](https://github.com/oroinc/crm-application)** [![Stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/oroinc/crm-application?style=social&color=white)](https://github.com/oroinc/crm-application/stargazers)  
  **Flexible CRM for marketing, sales, and customer service**, OSL-3.0 licensed. **Built on Symfony framework** — **strong test coverage and comprehensive developer documentation** [citation:10]. **Great user documentation, automated actions, email sync, and custom fields** [citation:10]. **Limitations: outdated frontend stack, complex initial setup, steep learning curve, and no mobile app** [citation:10]. **Best for B2B eCommerce companies that need CRM integrated with commerce**. 🏗️

- **[Krayin](https://github.com/krayin/laravel-crm)** [![Stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/krayin/laravel-crm?style=social&color=white)](https://github.com/krayin/laravel-crm/stargazers)  
  **Low-code CRM with enterprise-grade features**, MIT licensed. **Laravel-based** — **designed for businesses that want a modern, low-code CRM** [citation:3]. **The most accessible Laravel-based open-source CRM**. 🛠️

- **[Django-CRM](https://github.com/MicroPyramid/Django-CRM)** [![Stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/MicroPyramid/Django-CRM?style=social&color=white)](https://github.com/MicroPyramid/Django-CRM/stargazers)  
  **CRM for startups with sales pipeline management**, MIT licensed. **Django-based** — **analytical CRM with task management and email marketing** [citation:3][citation:11]. **Designed for easy customization and quick development** [citation:11]. **The most Pythonic open-source CRM**. 🐍

- **[DaybydayCRM](https://github.com/Bottelet/DaybydayCRM)** [![Stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/Bottelet/DaybydayCRM?style=social&color=white)](https://github.com/Bottelet/DaybydayCRM/stargazers)  
  **Open-source CRM to help you keep track of your daily workflow**, MIT licensed. **Track daily workflow with leads, tasks, and clients** [citation:3]. **PHP/Laravel-based** — **lightweight and focused** [citation:3]. **Best for solopreneurs and very small teams**. 📅

## ⚠️ Disclaimer

- This is a **community-curated** list — not exhaustive and not an endorsement. ℹ️
- **Real-year-one costs are significantly higher than advertised prices** — **Salesforce Pro Suite for 10 users costs $39,600–$74,600/year** with implementation and admin overhead [citation:5]. **HubSpot Starter-to-Professional jump is 6x** ($15 to $90/seat/month) with **no plan in between** [citation:5]. **Mandatory onboarding on Professional+ adds $1,500–$3,000** [citation:5].
- **Open-source CRM is not free** — **SuiteCRM and EspoCRM have zero license cost** but **require technical expertise to deploy, customize, and maintain** [citation:2]. **Open-source CRMs have hidden costs**: server costs, maintenance work, and the engineering talent to manage the system [citation:2]. **For very small organizations without technical staff, a SaaS CRM may be cheaper in practice**.
- **Zoho CRM Plus Starter is the most cost-effective all-in-one option** for small teams at **$20/user/month** with **no contract lock-in** [citation:7]. **HubSpot's free tier is the best starting point** for teams that want to try before committing [citation:6].
- **Always validate CRM fit with a free trial** before purchasing — **test with colleagues from different departments** to see how it works across situations [citation:12]. 🤝
